# ADR-PROC-006: Financial Boundaries Constitution

## Status
Frozen

## Mission
Define the exact separation between commercial commitments and financial obligations to permanently prevent Procurement from acting as a shadow accounting system.

---

## 1. The Core Financial Doctrine
- **Procurement Commits**
- **Finance Accounts**
- **Finance Pays**

**CRITICAL RULE**: Procurement must NEVER pay vendors, post journal entries, or create AP liabilities.

## 2. Expected Cost vs Actual Liability
This distinction is strictly frozen:
- **Procurement owns**: Expected Cost, Negotiated Price, Commercial Terms, Purchase Commitment.
- **Finance owns**: Actual Liability, Invoice Amount, Payment Amount, Accounting Entries.
  - *Example*: If PO = $10,000 and Invoice = $10,350:
    - Procurement tracks $10,000 as the commercial expectation.
    - Finance establishes $10,350 as the authoritative liability. Both are valid in their respective contexts.

## 3. Three-Way Match Ownership
- **Finance exclusively owns** the Three-Way Match Evaluation (PO vs. Receipt vs. Invoice).
- *Reason*: The matching output dictates AP liabilities and payments, which belong solely to Finance. Procurement merely supplies the supporting commitment and receipt facts to the downstream engine.

## 4. Invoice Ownership
- Procurement must **never own** the Invoice Lifecycle.
- States such as `Draft Invoice`, `Approved Invoice`, or `Paid Invoice` are strictly forbidden inside the CAP-PROCUREMENT bounded context.
- Invoices belong entirely to Finance. Procurement aggregates may only reference an `invoiceId` externally.

## 5. Payment Ownership
- **Finance exclusively owns**: Payment Scheduling, Payment Execution, and Payment Reconciliation.
- Procurement aggregates must never track Bank Accounts, Payment Batches, or Settlement Details.

## 6. Budget Ownership
- **Budget Ownership** is explicitly **OUT OF SCOPE** for CAP-PROCUREMENT v1.0.0.
- Budgeting belongs to Finance/Controlling. Procurement may eventually consume budget checks via ACLs, but it does not master budget balances.

## 7. Variance Ownership
Variances are split by operational nature:
- **Commercial Variances** (Price changes, Supplier negotiation, Delivery shortage) are owned by **Procurement**.
- **Financial Variances** (Invoice difference, Tax difference, Payment difference, Currency difference) are owned by **Finance**.

## 8. Event Ownership Classification
Events generated by Procurement are *Commercial Facts*, not accounting facts. Finance translates these into accounting impacts via ACLs.
- Procurement emits: `PurchaseOrderApproved`, `PurchaseOrderCancelled`, `GoodsReceiptCompleted`, `SupplierReturnApproved`.

## 9. Future Scope Protection
Explicitly **OUT OF SCOPE** for v1.0.0 (Reserved for CAP-FINANCE):
- Accounts Payable
- Invoice Processing
- Payment Runs
- Tax Engines
- General Ledger
- Cost Accounting
- Budget Planning

## 10. Discovery Certifications
- `CERT-PROC-011 Financial Boundary Integrity`: Guarantees that absolutely no accounting or ledger logic exists inside Procurement.
- `CERT-PROC-012 Commitment vs Liability Integrity`: Guarantees that Expected Cost ≠ Actual Liability, preserving strict ownership boundaries.
